Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Doppel does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and keeps nothing on any server. Your photos and videos are made on your phone and stay on your phone. The app has no internet access of its own. The only exception is the optional location stamp described below.

Who we are

Doppel: Dual Camera (“the app”) is published on Google Play by IlocosScript (“we”). Contact: support@ilocosscript.com.

Data we collect

None. The app does not request the Android internet permission, has no account or login, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Permissions and what they are used for

  • Camera. Used to show the live preview and to take photos and videos with the back and front cameras. Images are processed in memory on your phone.
  • Microphone. Used only while you record a video, to add sound to it. Audio is never recorded at any other time and is never sent anywhere.
  • Approximate location (optional). Requested only if you turn on the Location stamp in Settings. It is used to write the city or area onto the photos and videos you take. Approximate (city-level) location is enough; the app never asks for your precise location.

Location stamp

The location stamp is off by default. When you turn it on, the app reads your approximate location on your phone. To turn it into a place name such as “Laoag City, Ilocos Norte”, the app uses Android’s built-in geocoding service, which may send the coordinates to Google, the provider of that service, to look up the name. The app itself does not store your location or send it anywhere, and the place name only ends up in the photos and videos you choose to take. If no place name is available, the coordinates are stamped instead. Turn the stamp off at any time in Settings, or remove the location permission in Android settings.

The app does not ask for permission to read your photo library. It saves new photos and videos through Android’s standard media storage, and only reads back the items it created itself (to show the thumbnail of your last capture).

Data stored on your phone

  • Photos and videos you take are saved to the Pictures/Doppel and Movies/Doppel folders on your device, where your gallery app can see them. You control them like any other photo or video: you can share, move or delete them at any time.
  • Settings (such as layout, video quality and timer) are stored only in the app’s private storage. They are removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your own Google backup, under your Google account’s terms.

Purchases

If you buy the optional Doppel Pro upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Doppel Pro, so it can unlock the Pro features. Google’s handling of purchases is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version ever handles personal data, this page will say so before that version is released.
